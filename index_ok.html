Welcome

We are an Italian sim racing team, built around one idea: a virtual racing team should be run like a real one. Structured line-ups, a dedicated engineering group, defined race procedures and a debrief after every session.

We compete across multi-class endurance events, sprint championships and single-seater series — from short-format races to the classic twenty-four hour distances.

Stay updated by checking us out on social media.

UPDATE

Our endurance programme goes the full distance

Multi-driver crews running prototype and GT machinery over four, six, twelve and twenty-four hour distances, with full stint planning and live strategy control from the pit wall.

Every entry is prepared weeks in advance: car development, fuel and tyre strategy, driver rotation and contingency plans.

UPDATE

Our liveries, our identity

White, blue and red. Every car that leaves our garage carries the same identity across every class we race in, from GT3 machinery to single-seaters.

Livery design, race graphics, onboard content and social coverage are produced in-house — visibility that keeps working long after the chequered flag.
