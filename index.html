Who we are

A racing team
that happens
to be virtual

Vesta Sport is an Italian esports organisation built around one idea: a sim racing team should be run like a real one. Structured driver line-ups, a dedicated engineering group, defined race procedures and a debrief after every session.

Our crews compete across multi-class endurance events, sprint championships and single-seater series, from short-format races to the classic twenty-four hour distances. Every entry is prepared weeks in advance — car development, stint planning, fuel and tyre strategy, driver rotation and contingency plans.

Behind the drivers there is a full operations group: race engineers, strategists, spotters and media. It is the part nobody sees on the broadcast, and it is the part that decides results.

How we work

  • 01Preparation over talent. Pace is built in testing, not found on race day.
  • 02Data first. Every set-up decision is backed by telemetry and lap analysis.
  • 03Clean racing. Finishing is a strategy. Incident-free stints win championships.
  • 04One crew. Drivers, engineers and spotters are judged on the same result.
  • 05Long game. We develop drivers season after season, not race by race.

Operations

What happens
before the lights

A race weekend is the last five percent of the work. This is the rest.

01

Vehicle development

Set-up programmes built per circuit and per stint window, validated in long runs rather than single hot laps. Balance, tyre degradation and fuel sensitivity are mapped before the event.

02

Race strategy

Stint plans, pit windows, safety-car scenarios and driver rotation are modelled in advance and adjusted live by a dedicated strategist on the pit wall.

03

Driver coaching

Telemetry overlays, delta analysis and one-to-one review sessions. Every driver has a development plan with measurable targets across the season.

04

Spotting & race control

Dedicated spotters on every entry: traffic management in multi-class racing, gap calls, incident awareness and clear radio discipline for the full distance.

05

Media & broadcast

In-house liveries, race graphics, onboard content and social coverage that give drivers and partners visibility well beyond the chequered flag.
